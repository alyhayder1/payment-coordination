What We Do

Administrative and payment coordination services for independent professionals working with clients internationally.

01

Payment Coordination

Coordination of client payments for independent freelancers using supported payment infrastructure and arrangements.

02

Administrative Support

Administrative assistance related to payment records, transaction reconciliation and payment documentation.

03

International Payment Coordination

Support for independent freelancers who work with clients internationally and require suitable payment arrangements.

04

Transaction Records

Clear records of relevant payments, applicable service fees and amounts coordinated through the service.

How It Works

The underlying freelance relationship remains between the freelancer and their client.

1

Service Agreement

The independent freelancer agrees with their client on the scope, price and delivery of the service.

2

Client Payment

Where our service is used, the client makes the agreed payment through the available payment infrastructure.

3

Coordination

The payment is recorded and coordinated in accordance with the applicable platform and payment arrangements.

4

Freelancer Payout

The amount due to the freelancer is paid through the supported payout method after applicable fees.

5

Records

Relevant payment and transaction records are maintained for administration and reconciliation.

For Independent Freelancers

Our service is intended for independent professionals who already provide services directly to their own clients.

  • Agree your scope and pricing directly with your client.
  • Remain responsible for providing the underlying service.
  • Provide appropriate invoices or other required documentation.
  • Remain responsible for your own tax and legal obligations.
  • Use our service for payment coordination and related administration.

For Clients

Clients work directly with the relevant independent freelancer regarding the underlying service.

  • Agree the scope of work and price with the freelancer.
  • Review the applicable payment arrangements before making payment.
  • Make payments through the available payment infrastructure.
  • Contact the relevant freelancer regarding questions about the underlying service.
  • Contact us for payment-related administrative questions.
